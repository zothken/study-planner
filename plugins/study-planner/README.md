# study-planner (plugin)

One skill, **study-planner**: Claude builds and maintains your personal study planner as a live artifact —
course catalogue with ratings, clash-checked timetable, wishlist, progress against your examination
regulations, patch notes for every update. Imports from Stud.IP (dedicated helpers) and other campus systems.

- Setup guide (English): [skills/study-planner/README.md](skills/study-planner/README.md)
- Einrichtungsanleitung (Deutsch): [skills/study-planner/README.de.md](skills/study-planner/README.de.md)

Try it after installing: *“Build me a study planner for my programme”* / *„Erstelle mir einen Studienplaner.“*
